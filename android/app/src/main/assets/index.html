Stand at the start of the feed bunk and tap Start pen. Walk along the bunk and tap End pen at the far end. Pens that touch within the link distance join into one side of an alley.

Rows facing each other across an alley are detected automatically. Pens extend away from the alley, to half the distance to the next row behind them, or the default depth if none is found.

    Saved as YARD_MAP.TXT. Each pen line is PEN,id then four corners (bunk start, bunk end, back of end, back of start) as latitude, longitude, altitude. Files with only the two bunk points also open, and the back corners are worked out for you.

    Pair the receiver (for example the TOPGNSS TOP608BT) in Android Bluetooth settings first. The PIN is usually 0000 or 1234. Then pick it below.

    The app looks for a receiver that sends NMEA sentences (GGA, RMC) on a serial port. It tries each port at the common baud rates and connects to the first one that answers. Accuracy comes from the receiver itself, so an RTK receiver shows centimeter readings.
